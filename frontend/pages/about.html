About AttendSynth AI

Platform architecture, statistical synthesis methodology, generative AI integration, and ethical standards.

Abstract & Mission

AttendSynth AI is an enterprise-grade synthetic student engagement generator designed to accelerate the development, testing, and validation of academic early-warning systems. By modeling latent behavioral distributions and deriving correlated educational signals (attendance, LMS platform activity, assignment completion, academic performance), the platform produces realistic datasets without exposing confidential student Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

Research & Prototyping

Provides ethically unrestricted, statistically plausible cohorts for academic analytics benchmarking.

Absolute Privacy Protection

Complies with FERPA/GDPR principles by generating 100% artificial, non-re-identifiable student profiles.

Grounded Generative AI

Combines Google Gemini 3.8 Flash with deterministic Python calculations to prevent hallucinations.

Data Generation Methodology

Latent behavioral prior hierarchy.

Each student is first sampled into an underlying behavioral attendance profile drawn from user-specified priors:

Profile Attendance Range Default Mix
Regular82% – 100%40%
Moderate63% – 85%30%
Irregular42% – 65%20%
Low18% – 45%10%
Profile → Attendance → Engagement Components → Academic Score → Risk Score

Early Warning Risk Formula

Deterministic heuristic (Not an ML model).

Risk is computed transparently using an inverted weighted positive index:

risk_score = 100 − (
  0.35 × attendance_percentage +
  0.25 × engagement_score +
  0.25 × assignment_completion_pct +
  0.15 × academic_performance_score
)
Low: < 35 Medium: 35 – 59 High: ≥ 60

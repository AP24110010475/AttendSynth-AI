Risk Category Definitions

Institutional threshold criteria and intervention guidelines.

Low Risk (Score < 35)

Students maintain high attendance (≥80%), complete most coursework on time, and exhibit active platform engagement. Routine progress monitoring is recommended.

Medium Risk (Score 35–59)

Inconsistent attendance patterns or moderate LMS interaction. Proactive automated reminders and mid-term check-ins prevent escalation to academic probation.

High Risk (Score ≥ 60)

Critically deficient attendance (<50%), severely lagging assignment completion, or low engagement scores. Immediate academic counseling and faculty intervention required.

Mathematical Formulation

Deterministic weighted scoring equation.

risk_score = 100 − (
  0.35 × attendance_percentage +
  0.25 × engagement_score +
  0.25 × assignment_completion_pct +
  0.15 × academic_performance_score
)
  • Attendance Weight (35%): Highest weight reflecting empirical impact on graduation.
  • Engagement & Assignment (50% combined): Real-time behavioral progress indicators.
  • Academic Performance (15%): Cumulative assessment score.
